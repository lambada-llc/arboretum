Selfie path

The selfie show draws its own tree and slides the board's 25 × 25 window over it, one pixel a frame, a strip of 25 rows at a time from the top down. Its tree is the one the build generates from selfie.lamb, a smaller tree that gives the program back. Below: its path over that tree, frame by frame.

Strips alternate rightward and leftward, with a drop where tree shows at both ends of it, and the window jumps only between views that show nothing. Edges alternate between two greys by row, so that moving along one shows which way the window goes.
